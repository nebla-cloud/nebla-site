# Open source

Nebla's core is licensed under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](https://github.com/nebla-cloud/nebla/blob/main/LICENSE). That covers the panel, the API, the CLI and everything a homelab or a small team needs.

## What the AGPL means for you

- You can run Nebla for any purpose, including at work and for paying customers.
- You can read and change the code.
- If you run a modified Nebla as a service that other people use over a network, you have to offer them the source of your changes under the same licence.

Running an unmodified Nebla for yourself or your customers asks nothing extra of you.

## What's free

All of the core, with no limit on nodes, clusters, projects or users. Features don't move from the free core into a paid tier.

## What's paid

A reseller module for hosting businesses: billing integration, customer self-signup, white-label branding, usage metering and a reseller hierarchy. It is a separate build on top of the core, under a commercial licence, and it is still in development. It pays for work on the core.

## The contributor licence agreement

Contributors sign a [CLA](https://github.com/nebla-cloud/nebla/blob/main/CLA.md) before their first pull request is merged. A bot asks you on the pull request and you sign by leaving a comment.

The CLA lets the project ship your contribution in both the AGPL core and the commercial module. Your contribution stays available under the AGPL in the open repository.

## Contributing

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/nebla-cloud/nebla/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) for the development setup, tests and commit style. Bug reports and feature ideas go in [GitHub issues](https://github.com/nebla-cloud/nebla/issues). Security problems go through [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/nebla-cloud/nebla/security), never public issues.
